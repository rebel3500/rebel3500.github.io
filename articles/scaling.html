Scaling Read-Heavy Systems Without Losing Consistency

Many modern platforms are overwhelmingly read-heavy, especially directory-style applications with filtering and search.

The Core Problem

As traffic grows, read queries begin to dominate system resources. Without proper design, this leads to increased latency and stale data.

Strategies That Work

  • Read-optimized database schemas
  • Selective caching instead of blanket caching
  • Precomputed query results for common filters
  • Efficient indexing on location and availability

Balancing Freshness and Speed

Real-time systems require a balance between consistency and performance. Instead of full synchronization, targeted updates and partial invalidation often provide better results.
